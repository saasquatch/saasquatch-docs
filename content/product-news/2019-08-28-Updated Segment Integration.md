With our new V2 Segment integration, communication between SaaSquatch and Segment is now a two-way street; SaaSquatch can update your users in Segment and Segment can update your users in SaaSquatch. With Segment available as a source, you can accomplish Identification, Attribution and Conversion via Segment!
        
This new integration is in beta and opt-in only while we ensure it is working optimally, so if you are interested in making use of it along with your existing SaaSquatch program please contact the Success Team. If you would like to find out how SaaSquatch can improve upon your existing Segment implementation, click here to schedule a product demo.
