We’ve updated our participants section in the SaaSquatch portal to provide improved data accessibility and a better user experience. 

A new key feature that you’ll notice is the option to **DELETE a user**. This is part of our recent changes to ensure that the SaaSquatch platform remains GDPR compliant. You can can read more about our GDPR updates [here](/features/participant-deletion). 

Updated portal pages include: 
* All Users list
* Participants list
* User Profile Page
