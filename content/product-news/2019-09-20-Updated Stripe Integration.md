It is now possible to automatically fulfill credit rewards through Stripe in [Growth Automation](/growth/ga-101/) programs. As the integration is fully automated, once set up the SaaSquatch system will forward any qualifying credits directly to Stripe to be credited into your users’ accounts. 

For more information on how to configure your SaaSquatch programs and connect it with Stripe, please refer to our [Stripe Guide](/developer/stripe-v2-install-guide/). 

As this integration is currently in beta, please reach out to our [Success Team](mailto:success@referralsaasquatch.com) to activate the integration in your account, or to answer any questions you may have about getting set up with Stripe!
