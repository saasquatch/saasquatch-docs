We’ve updated the SaaSquatch widget editor to help you supercharge your referral and loyalty programs and hit your goals faster. 

#### Key Features:

__A user-friendly visual builder:__ Our intuitive interface allows you to easily add, move or remove widget components, while the Preview mode shows you how your programs will look across multiple devices.

__Fully stocked template library:__ Our library of ready-to-use widget templates can get you up and running quickly. You can also create a tailor-made program to suit your needs by selecting from a myriad of individual components.

__Flexible under the hood:__ Our widget editor now allows in-line CSS editing through the HTML editor along with the ability to build your own web components. Or, harness the power of JSONata to add advanced conditional content.

If you’ve been wanting to spruce up your referral and loyalty experiences, now’s the time! Login to your dashboard to get started, or learn more about the features [here](https://www.saasquatch.com/widget-editor/).
