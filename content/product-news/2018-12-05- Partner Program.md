    <p>
      As SaaSquatch continues to expand our
      <a href="/growth/saasquatch-ga">Growth Automation platform</a
      >, you can now engage your business affiliates with our all encompassing
      <bold>Partner Program</bold>!
    </p>
    <p>Partner Programs enable you to generate dynamic percentage rewards OR
      static pre-configured reward values. This supports commission rewards
      where reward amounts will vary.
    </p>
    <p>
      You can also now define your own metrics to update a user’s information
      fields, and moderate actions that have been previously performed; e.g.
      when a refund for a purchase is issued, any rewards associated with that
      purchase can be canceled.
    </p>
    <p>
      Additionally, participant profile pages have been updated, including the
      display of the formatted reward <code>name</code> which will now
      distinguish the reward in a human readable format (i.e. cash from credit
      rewards).
    </p>
    <h4>New Partner Widget</h4>
    <p>
      Finally, a brand new <bold>Partner Widget</bold> is now available to boost
      the visibility of your program and show reward balances to your
      affiliates.
      <img src="https://www.saasquatch.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Partner-Program-Widget.png" alt="pp-widget"/>
    </p>
    <p>
      If you’re interested in getting started with a [Partner Program](mailto:
      support@referralsaasquatch.com?subject=Partner Program) and would like
      some tips and tricks, your Support team is happy to help!
    </p>
