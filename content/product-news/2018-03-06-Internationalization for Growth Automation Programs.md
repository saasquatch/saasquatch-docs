    <p>
      <bold>Upload your own translations for program emails and widgets based on
        locale.</bold>
      This new feature is now available for all Growth Automation programs and
      provides the ability to upload your own customized sets of translations
      for both program emails and widgets.
    </p>
    <p>
      From the “Advanced” tab in your program configuration/set-up, upload and
      download your translation sets using simple zip files, either
      individually, or in bulk.
    </p>
    <p>
      To learn more about this new feature or Growth Automation programs,
      <a href="mailto:sales@referralsaasquatch.com">contact our sales team</a>.
    </p>
    <p>
      Already a SaaSquatch customer? This feature is currently in Beta. Please
      <a href="mailto:support@referralsaasquatch.com?subject=Growth%20Automation">contact our support team</a>
      to learn more about how to enroll in our Growth Automation Beta program.
    </p>
