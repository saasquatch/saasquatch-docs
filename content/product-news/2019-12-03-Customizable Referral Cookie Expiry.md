__Update June 10,2020:__ Configurable cookie expiry works with [1st party cookies](/developer/squatchjs/cookies/)

One of the ways we link Referred Users to Referrers is by placing a cookie in the Referred User’s browser when a sharelink is clicked. Traditionally, this cookie has had a one year expiry date, meaning that it would cease functioning one year after first being created.

With our latest update, you can now specify a custom amount of time before the cookie expires. This is great for creating limited-time offers, gating participants or ensuring that referrals don’t get lost to time by setting it to the maximum length of one year.

This feature is now available in all [Growth Automation programs](/program/library/) that use referral cookies: the Referral Program, the Tiered Referral Program and the Partner Program.
