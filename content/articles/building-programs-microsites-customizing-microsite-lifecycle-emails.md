## Add a new email
1. Open the microsite editor.
2. Click __Email Templates__ in the left sidebar menu.
3. Click __Create new email template__ in the Emails tab.
4. Enter your email template ID.
5. Click __Create__.
    - You’ll be redirected to the email editor.
6. Make your changes.
    - __Note:__ If you want someone on your team to receive a copy of each email that's sent to a participant, then you can adjust the __BCC field__ setting in the email editor. 
8. Click __Save__.

## Edit an email template
1. Open the microsite editor.
2. Click __Email Templates__ in the left sidebar menu.
3. Click the name of the email you want to edit.
    - You’ll be redirected to the email editor.
4. Make your changes.
    - __Note:__ If you want someone on your team to receive a copy of each email that's sent to a participant, then you can adjust the __BCC field__ setting in the email editor. 
5. Click __Save__.

## Replace a lifecycle email
You can decide which email templates are used for the lifecycle emails by adjusting your microsite settings in the Admin Portal.

1. Return to the __Content__ tab in the Admin Portal.
2. In the Microsite card, click __Edit Settings__.
3. Under the Participant Login heading, find the Lifecycle Emails section and click __Change Templates__ on the right.
4. Use the dropdown menus to select the email you want to send out for verification or password resets.
5. Click __Save__.
