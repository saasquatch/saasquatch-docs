> This guide only covers how to change the display name of your SaaSquatch program. To change your Program ID, please contact the <a href="mailto:support@saasquatch.com">Support Team</a>.

1. Head to the 'Programs' tab in your SaaSquatch tenant.
![Programs Tab](/assets/images/contentful/Screenshot_2021-03-05_1.28.05_PM_5OfWSAU0kyXdYBL8UwT8aF.png)

2. Click on "Manage" under the sub-navigation.

3. Once you have located the program you want to rename, click on the pencil icon to the right-hand side of the program's name. 
![Manage Programs](/assets/images/contentful/Screenshot_2021-03-05_1.31.36_PM_2bHeJleIn8kUCg75eqz2Zp.png)

3. You should now be able to modify the disaply name of your program. 
![Edit Program Name](/assets/images/contentful/Screenshot_2021-03-05_1.33.30_PM_6FSxSixYabYiuatD19GYH5.png)

4. Click on the orange checkmark button to save your edits.
![Program Name Edited](/assets/images/contentful/Screenshot_2021-03-05_1.37.54_PM_LVJ7PJBU6ACoSJuW9iSBB.png)
