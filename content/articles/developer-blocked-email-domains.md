The SaaSquatch Security Management System supports [flagging](/developer/referral-security/#suspicious-email-address-domains) participants who have signed up with an email address from a suspicious domain.

### Built-in Blocked Domain List
By default the SaaSquatch system will check referrals against a built-in list of suspicious email domains. This list is sourced from a constantly updated public domain, [crowd-sourced list](https://github.com/martenson/disposable-email-domains) of disposable and temporary email domains. 

### Custom Blocked Domains
In addition to the built-in list of suspicious email domains you can choose to add additional domains.

1. Navigate to the **Security** tab on the [Settings page](/success/navigating-the-portal/#settings).

2. In the **Security Options** window select the **Global** tab.

3. Make sure that the **Suspicious Email Domains** Criteria is enabled.

4. Click on **Manage custom blocked domains**:

![Security Options](/assets/images/contentful/Security_Options_n6rurzm9fqgiOiKOw6kEw.png)

5. Add/Remove email domains that you would like to block in addition to our [Built-in Blocked Domain List](#built-in-blocked-domain-list).

![Blocked Domains](/assets/images/contentful/Blocked_Domains_3wd8YFPRMAoaIccsgwkGS2.png)

6. Save your changes.
