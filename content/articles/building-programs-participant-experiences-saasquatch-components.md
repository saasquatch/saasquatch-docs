Components are the individual building blocks, like hero images and task cards, that make up your widget or microsite. All of our current templates are powered by our mint and bedrock components libraries. 

The table below shows the components you can use to build your participant experience, as well as how we group them in our visual editor for widgets and microsites. If you want to learn more about our widget and microsite templates and component libraries, then check out our doc on [participant experiences](/features/end-user-experiences).

> Important: If your widget still uses our legacy vanilla components package, then you won't see the categories and components below. [Manually upgrade](/features/end-user-experiences#mint-components) to our current mint components library to begin using our newest features and participant experiences.
