Launch a referral program to accelerate your growth by leveraging the advocacy of your top customers. Reward existing customers for referring you to new business, and acquire new leads at a fraction of the cost of traditional marketing channels. 

Integrate a branded referral widget directly into your app or website to deliver a seamless experience for your customers. 

### Example:

A CRM platform launches a referral program that rewards existing customers with a $100 invoice credit when they refer a friend who becomes a paying customer. The referred friend also receives a $100 discount on their first month.  

### Features:

* Reward based on custom user criteria and actions, such as when the referred friend makes a purchase or signs up for a subscription.  
* Build a multi-objective referral sequence with a variety of conversion goals.
* Create reward tiers for the best ROI.
* Set a maximum number of rewards a customer can get.
* Choose if one or both parties will receive a reward.
* Choose under what conditions the referral reward will be cancelled. 
* Target specific customer segments.
* Customize share links and codes for referrals.
* Customize emails for starting a referral and when rewards are given.
* Customize widgets for both sides of a referral to monitor referrals.
* Customize social messaging.
