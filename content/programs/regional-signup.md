Reward new signups in a specific region to accelerate expansion into new markets, or strengthen your user base in existing areas. 

### Example:

A ride-sharing company is trying to expand into new markets and increase adoption across the country. 

The company launches a Regional Signup Program that offers a $15 credit to any new user in the state of Arizona. 

### Features:

* Set the location(s) where a first time signup will trigger a reward.
* Specify the reward earned when signup is completed.
* Customize the email to notify users when they get a reward.
